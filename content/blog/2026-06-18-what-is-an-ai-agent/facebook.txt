You keep hearing 'AI agent' everywhere — but what does it actually mean for someone running a small business? Here's the simplest way to think about it: a regular AI chatbot answers your questions. An AI agent takes a goal and goes and does the work.

Picture this: instead of asking 'how do I follow up with customers after a quote?', you tell the agent to go through your inbox, find every customer who got a quote in the last two weeks and hasn't replied, and draft a follow-up for each one. You review the drafts. You click send. What used to take 45 minutes takes 10.

You stay in charge the whole time — a well-built agent shows you what it's doing and asks before anything important happens. Think of it as a very organized assistant who lays everything out on your desk and says, 'Here's what I've prepared — want me to go ahead?'
