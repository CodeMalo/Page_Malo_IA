Jeff Bezos made a bold claim at a recent tech conference: AI won't kill jobs — it will actually create a labor shortage. More work, not less. And he's not just guessing. History backs him up every time a major technology arrived, from tractors to the internet, new industries and job titles showed up that nobody saw coming.

The logic is straightforward. When it gets cheaper and faster to do things, people do more things. A small bakery that can suddenly handle twice the orders doesn't lay people off — it grows, and growth means hiring. Bezos argues AI removes the bottlenecks that stop businesses from expanding in the first place.

That said, the article doesn't pretend this is guaranteed. Smart people disagree, and the honest answer is we'll see how fast things shift. But one thing is already true today: AI is making it possible for smaller teams to do what used to take much bigger ones. 🙌
