A practical, plain-language starter guide that helps small business owners adopt AI in just 30 minutes. It cuts through the confusion of choosing tools by giving you a clear system: which AI tool to use for which task, ready-to-copy prompts you can apply today, and a structured 7-day plan to put it all into action. The guide covers the five core AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, and DeepSeek), explains when free versions are enough versus when to upgrade, walks through automation with tools like Zapier and Make.com, and shows how to calculate the real ROI of every workflow you build. Written for business owners, not engineers.
